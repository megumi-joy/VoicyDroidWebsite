Privacy Policy for Magic Balls Adventure

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

1. Overview

Magic Balls Adventure (the "App") is a ball-rolling arcade game. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of data when you use our App.

2. Data Collection and Use

The App sends basic gameplay telemetry (score and game state events) to our backend service to help us understand how the game is played and improve it. This data is not linked to your identity: the App does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, contact list, or any other personally identifiable information, and does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

3. Permissions

The App requests device sensor access (accelerometer/gyroscope) to support tilt-based controls, and network access to send the gameplay telemetry described above. No other device permissions are requested.

4. Security

Gameplay telemetry is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our backend. We do not sell or share this data with third parties.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through the Google Play Store developer contact info.
