Privacy Policy for Электричка (Elektrichka)

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

1. Overview

Электричка (the "App") is a visual novel game. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

2. Data Collection and Use

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It does not require an account, does not connect to any backend service, and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. All game progress is stored locally on your device.

3. Permissions

The App does not request any device permissions beyond what is required to run.

4. Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit any data, there is no personal information at risk from our App's operation.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through the Google Play Store developer contact info.
